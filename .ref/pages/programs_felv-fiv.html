Creamsicle's FeLV/FIV Test Program

Early testing saves lives.

Every cat who comes through our doors is tested for feline leukemia virus and feline immunodeficiency virus, so care can start sooner and adopters know exactly what they are bringing home.

Meet the catsMicrochip program

Why we test

Named for a cat who deserved answers.

FeLV and FIV are viruses that affect cats. Many cats who carry them live long, comfortable lives with the right care, and knowing a cat's status lets us plan for that from day one. Testing also protects the other cats in our rooms.

We named the program after Creamsicle, a sweet orange and white cat. Testing early means fewer surprises for the cat and for the family who takes them home.

Every cat, every time

Cats and kittens are tested when they arrive, before they are listed for adoption.

Results you can ask about

We will walk you through what a positive or negative result means for the cat you are meeting.

Care after adoption

If a cat needs follow up care, we help you plan it with your own veterinarian.

Keep it going

Testing is funded by donations.

Every test kit, every vet visit and every follow up comes from people who care about cats in Stokes County. If you would like to help the next cat get answers sooner, we would be glad to put your gift to work.
