Who we are

A better future for the homeless pets of Stokes County.

Friends of the Stokes Shelter Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that runs a no-kill animal adoption center at 1111 Dodgetown Road in the Meadows community, between Walnut Cove and Danbury.

Meet the animalsVisit us

Built by neighbors

The building was paid for by the community, with $450,000 raised over six years.

2,058 animals saved

Pulled from the Stokes County Animal Shelter since we opened, as of May 1, 2026.

No government funding

We receive no local or government money. Every dollar comes from private donors.

One shelter, one source

We only pull animals from the county shelter and do not take owner surrenders.

Why the shelter exists

Our goal is to give homeless pets a fur-ever home and to relieve overcrowding at the Stokes County Animal Control shelter, which reduces euthanizations caused by a lack of space.

We are an adoption center, not an open intake shelter. Every animal in our care is pulled from the Stokes County Animal Shelter, and we do not take animals from the public. We are a separate organization from the SCAC shelter on Sizemore Road.

The center was completed through private donations, with $450,000 raised over six years by people in this community. We receive no local or other government funding and rely 100 percent on private donations to operate.
