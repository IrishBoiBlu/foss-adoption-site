Cooper is a handsome boy with soulful eyes and a heart full of love. This sweet guy is the perfect mix of playful energy and gentle affection, making him an amazing companion for just about any home. Cooper loves spending time with his people-whether that's going for walks, playing outside, or simply sitting close for some well-earned snuggles. He has a curious, friendly personality and is always ready to greet you with a wagging tail and that adorable "did you call me" face. At his age, Cooper is past the tiny puppy stage but still young enough to bring lots of fun and excitement into your life. He's eager to learn, wants to please, and would thrive with a family who will continue to guide him and shower him with love. If you're looking for a loyal, loving, and oh-so-cute companion, Cooper might just be your perfect match.
