Adoption process

Three steps, one very good day.

Applications are reviewed by people who know the animals, not by an algorithm. Here is exactly what happens between your first click and the ride home.

Start an application336-914-9270
  1. 01

    Apply

    Fill out an adoption application. We ask for basic information, your vet history and two personal references. You can start from the Apply Now button on any pet profile.

  2. 02

    We review

    Processing usually takes at least three business days. Please let your references know to expect a call from us, which makes the process faster.

  3. 03

    Meet & greet

    Once your application is approved we schedule a meet and greet. If the animal is a good fit, you can adopt that same day.

Adoption questions

Can I bring my dog to meet the pet I am interested in?+

Yes, and we highly encourage it. A meet and greet with your current dog before you bring home a new pet gives everyone a calmer start.

Where do I apply?+

Start from any pet profile on our adoptable dogs and cats pages and use the Apply Now button on the kennel card. Applications go straight into our ShelterLuv system.

What is an adoption package?+

It gives you time to get an appointment with your own vet without missing any necessary preventions. Ask us about it when you apply.

What if the pet is not a good fit?+

Tell us before the meet and greet ends. We would much rather help you find the right match than send a pet home with the wrong one. Call 336-914-9270 and we will talk it through.

Ready to meet them?

Visits are by appointment only. Call ahead and we will set aside a quiet room and someone who knows the animal.
