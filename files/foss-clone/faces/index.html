Outreach

F.A.C.E.S of FOSS.

Our outreach program takes the shelter into the community. In 2025 the F.A.C.E.S team grew and visited nursing homes and schools around Stokes County.

Invite F.A.C.E.SEmail the program(opens in a new tab)

Where we go

Animal visits that do some good.

Nursing homes

Companion visits that bring a calm, friendly animal into a shared room.

Schools

Classroom visits that teach children how to meet a dog or cat kindly.

Community groups

Visits for organizations that want to learn about animal welfare in Stokes County.

How can this program help your organization?

Call 336-914-9270 or email fossservicesmanager@gmail.com to learn how F.A.C.E.S of FOSS can work with your school, care home or community group.
