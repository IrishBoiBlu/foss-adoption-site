Planned giving

A legacy that outlives all of us.

Planned gifts support the rescue and rehoming of animals in need for decades to come. They can also carry tax advantages for you.

Give to the endowment fund(opens in a new tab)Talk it through

Ways to give

Common planned gift options.

Bequests

Name Friends of the Stokes Shelter in your will, with specific information about what you want to give.

Life insurance

Name FOSS as a beneficiary, or donate the accumulated value of a whole life policy.

Retirement plans

Name FOSS as a beneficiary, or as a recipient of a percentage of the proceeds.

Qualified charitable distributions

Transfer funds from an IRA directly to FOSS. It may count toward your required minimum distribution.

Trusts

Revocable or irrevocable trusts let you manage funds and distributions for your beneficiaries.

Appreciated securities

Transfer stock and take an income tax deduction for the fair market value, avoiding capital gains tax.

We recommend that you seek legal or financial planning advice, then send the appropriate documentation and contact the shelter so we can make sure your wishes are followed.

The FOSS Endowment Fund

Established in 2022, invested in perpetuity.

The FOSS Endowment Fund was established in 2022 with the Winston-Salem Foundation serving as trustee. The principal balance stays invested in perpetuity while the interest is available for ongoing operating expenses like animal feeding, veterinarian care and adoption preparation.

By giving to the endowment, a donor becomes part of caring for homeless dogs and cats at FOSS forever, through the interest earned on the investment.
