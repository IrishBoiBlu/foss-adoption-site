Our story

A small shelter with a very big heart.

Friends of the Stokes Shelter exists to rescue homeless domestic animals, to help them find new homes, to serve as a community resource, and to give pet owners the information they need about animal health and welfare.

We take in the dogs and cats the county shelter cannot hold, care for them, and get to know each one well enough to match them with the right family. Along the way we run programs for spay and neuter support, microchipping and health testing.

Programs

New programs, thanks to good neighbors.

Local partners fund the care that adoptions alone cannot cover. Together they keep more pets healthy, and more families whole.

Sauratown Dental Microchip Program

A tiny chip, a lifetime of protection. Free microchips that reunite lost pets with their families, in partnership with Sauratown Dental and King Veterinary Hospital.

Learn more

Creamsicle's FeLV/FIV Test Program

Early testing saves lives. Cats are tested for FeLV and FIV so they can start care sooner, in honor of our sweet Creamsicle.

Learn more

Margo T. Petrie Spaying & Neutering Foundation

Spay and neuter support that stops unwanted litters before they begin, and keeps pets in the homes they already have.

Learn more

With thanks to the Eubank family, Sauratown Dental, King Veterinary Hospital and Purina.
