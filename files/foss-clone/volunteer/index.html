Get involved

Love animals? Have some free time?

Volunteers average 850 hours a month at the adoption center. They walk dogs, socialize cats, help at events and keep the place calm and clean.

Ask for an applicationVolunteer portal(opens in a new tab)

Why it matters

Day to day operations would not happen without volunteers.

Do you love animals and want to make a difference in your community? Join the volunteer team and get out of the house for a few hours at the FOSS Animal Adoption Center.

Volunteers are a vital part of the Friends of the Stokes Shelter and help in many different ways. It is a good way to give back to your community while building friendships and having fun.

Dog enrichment

Walks, play time and quiet handling for dogs who need it.

Cat enrichment

Sitting with cats, brushing, toys and gentle socialization.

Cleaning and care

Kennels, laundry, dishes and the daily upkeep of the center.

Administrative help

Paperwork, phone calls and keeping the office organized.

Community outreach

Events, school visits and representing FOSS around the county.

Fundraising

Helping with events like the Putts for Pets golf tournament.

The ground rules

What we ask of volunteers.

  • Volunteer hours are offered every day of the year.
  • Volunteers must be at least 15 to work independently, with adequate staff or senior volunteer supervision.
  • Children ages 12 to 14 may volunteer with a trained parent or legal guardian. Both register as volunteers.
  • Every volunteer completes the mandatory orientation and the online Fear Free Shelter Training course.

Getting started

Ask for a volunteer application and we will send the orientation details. Current volunteers can log hours in the Volgistics portal.
